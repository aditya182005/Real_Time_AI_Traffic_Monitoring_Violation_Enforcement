# Your own photos and videos

Drop traffic photos (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.bmp`, `.webp`) and videos (`.mp4`,
`.avi`, `.mov`, `.mkv`) here. They then appear in the **Analyze footage** page
under "Or pick ... already in the project", so you do not have to upload them
each time.

Tips:

* Use footage from a **fixed** camera. Clips of 10-60 seconds are quickest.
* Only add footage you have the right to use. Large files make the repository
  heavy; add them to `.gitignore` or keep them out of commits if you do not
  want to share them.
* Steps and expected results: [docs/CHECKING_OUTPUTS.md](../../docs/CHECKING_OUTPUTS.md).
